The appellant relies on Municipal Corporation of the City of Ahmedabad v. State of Gujarat, [1973] 1 S.C.R. 1, for the proposition that a municipal body derives its powers from statute.

Reliance is also placed on National Buildings Construction Corporation v. Pritam Singh Gill, [1973] 1 S.C.R. 40.

The respondent cites Imaginary Holdings v. Union of India, [1973] 1 S.C.R. 9999, which holds that all contracts are void.
