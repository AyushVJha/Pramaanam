    Method

    Each citation goes through four stages. A stage that cannot be completed is reported as such; it is never reported as a pass.

    1. Extraction

      Citations are located in the draft: neutral (2023 INSC 11), SCR, SCC, AIR and SCC OnLine, with the case name and any quotation printed beside them. Footnotes in Word files are read.

    2. Resolution

      The citation number must lead to exactly one judgment. A neutral or SCR number is a proof by itself. An SCC or AIR number counts only when at least two other judgments print the same pairing of number and case.

    3. Comparison

      Both parties in the case name must fit the judgment and the year must agree. Each quotation is matched word for word against the text of the judgment, which detects a single dropped “not”.

    4. Report

      One status per citation, the reason in a sentence, and the judgment's own words beside the draft's. The report can be downloaded as an annexure for the file.

    A green status is given only on proof. Verified means the number, the name and the text agree. Anything less is marked for a person to examine. An automated language-model check, where enabled, can lower a result but cannot raise one.

    Statuses

    StatusMeaning
    Verified The number identifies the judgment, the name and year agree, and any quotation is word for word. This does not say that the judgment supports the proposition it is cited for.
    Needs review Not proven, or close but not exact: a near-match quotation, one party that differs, or a match on names alone. A person should examine it.
    MisquoteName mismatchWrong paragraph Issued only when the judgment is identified by its number and the quotation or name is clearly tied to that citation.
    Scan noise in source The quotation matches except where the judgment's text appears to contain a scanning error. Compare with the printed page.
    Not in corpus No matching judgment in the dataset. This is not a finding that the case does not exist: it may be a High Court decision or a report series that cannot be mapped.
    Cannot verify The check could not run, for example because the stored judgment has no text.

The text of older scanned judgments contains errors, about 2% of characters on a 40-page sample transcribed by the project itself (not verified by a human reviewer). That is why near matches go to review and not to an accusation.

    Privacy and limits

    Storage
    The file is read into a temporary location, checked and deleted. There is no account, no history, no tracking and no third-party script.
    Use of AI
    The checks are exact comparisons against the judgments. An optional language-model second opinion exists, is switched off on this site, and can only lower a result.
    Not covered
    Whether a judgment supports the argument it is cited for; High Court and tribunal decisions; short forms such as “supra” and “ibid”; headers, footers and text boxes in Word files.
    Status
    Preview release. Accuracy is still being measured. This is an automated check of citations and not legal advice; verify against an authorised source.
