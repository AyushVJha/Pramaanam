    § 2

    Four tests, in the order a careful junior would apply them.

    1. i
      (2019) 7 SCC 422023 INSC 11AIR 1960 SC 137

      Find

      Every citation in the draft, with the case name and any quotation printed beside it. Footnotes in Word files are read too.

    2. ii
      2023 INSC 11→one judgment

      Prove

      The number must lead to exactly one judgment. SCC and AIR numbers count only when at least two other judgments print the same pairing.

    3. iii
      “…Dr Singhvi is not that there are concurrent findings…”

      Compare

      Both parties must fit, the year must agree, and each quotation is matched word for word, down to a single dropped “not”.

    4. iv
      VerifiedNeeds reviewMisquote

      Report

      One status per citation, the reason in a sentence, and an annexure you can place on the file.

    A machine may raise a flag. It may never give a green tick on a guess.

    Verified means proven by the number, the name and the text. Anything less goes to a person.

    § 3

    Plain words, and never “fake” when the answer is simply unknown.

    Verified
    The number proves the judgment, the name and year match, and any quotation is word for word. It does not say the judgment supports your argument.
    Needs review
    Not proven, or close but not exact: a near-match quotation, one party that differs, a match on names alone. A person should look.
    Misquote · Name mismatch · Wrong paragraph
    Said only when the case is proven by its number and the quotation or name is clearly tied to that citation.
    Scan noise in source
    The quotation matches except for what looks like a scanning error in the judgment's text. Compare with the printed page.
    Not in corpus
    No matching judgment in the dataset. Not a finding that it is fake: it may be a High Court case or a report we cannot map.
    Cannot verify
    The check could not run, for example because the stored judgment has no text.

    § 5

    Your draft stays yours.

    Not stored. The file is read into a temporary place, checked and deleted. No account, no history, no tracking, no third-party scripts or fonts.

    No AI on your text by default. The checks are exact comparisons against the judgments. An optional AI second opinion exists, is off on this site, and could only ever lower a result.

    What it does not check. Whether the judgment supports your argument; High Court and tribunal cases; short forms such as “supra” and “ibid”; headers, footers and text boxes in Word files.

    Not legal advice. An automated check of citations, in preview. Verify against an authorised source. A lawyer decides.

    § 6

    Questions a careful advocate asks first.

    Does “Verified” mean the judgment supports my argument?

    No. Verified means the citation leads to the judgment, the parties and year match, and any quotation appears word for word. Whether the judgment holds what it is cited for is a question of law, and it stays with you.

    A citation says “Not in corpus”. Is the case fake?

    Not necessarily. The corpus holds Supreme Court of India judgments from the open CC-BY-4.0 dataset. A High Court or tribunal decision, or a report series that cannot be mapped to a judgment, will also show as not in corpus. Pramaanam never calls a citation fake.

    Is my draft stored, or used to train anything?

    No. The file is read into a temporary place, checked and deleted. There is no account and no history, and nothing is sent to a third party. The optional AI second opinion is switched off on this site.

    Which citation formats are read?

    Neutral citations (2023 INSC 11), SCR, SCC, AIR and SCC OnLine, with the case name beside them, in Word, PDF or plain text, footnotes included. Short forms such as “supra”, “ibid” and “id.” are not checked, and nor are headers, footers or text boxes in Word files.

    Why does a near-identical quotation go to review and not to “Misquote”?

    The text of older judgments comes from scanned pages and carries errors. When the difference could be the scan and not the draft, Pramaanam asks a person to compare with the printed page instead of making an accusation.

    Can the report go on the file?

    Yes. “Download annexure” gives a dated page listing each citation, its status and the reason. It is an automated check and not legal advice; verify against an authorised source.
